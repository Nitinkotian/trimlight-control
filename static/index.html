Your permanent lights, designed on your computer.

Create patterns, preview them on a house, schedule holidays and back everything up. The app runs on this computer and talks to your controller through Trimlight's official cloud API.

Connect your Trimlight account

Three steps, about two minutes. You only do this once.

  1. Get your API credentials from Trimlight

    Sign in on Trimlight's credentials page with the email and password you use in the Trimlight Edge phone app (or an emailed code). It gives you a Client ID and a Client Secret.

    Open Trimlight credentials page ↗

  2. Paste them here

  3. Connect

    The app checks the credentials with Trimlight, then saves them on this computer.

🔒 Your Client ID and Secret are saved only in the .env file next to this app and never leave this computer except to sign requests to Trimlight. You never type your Trimlight password into this app.
